# AfriShield Marketing Ops Plugin

**Inherits from:** `afrishield-core`

## Purpose

Marketing operations automation for AfriShield -- cybersecurity, fraud detection, AI SEO, and EA services for African markets. This plugin provides skills that connect HubSpot CRM, Firecrawl, Gmail, and Google Calendar to produce actionable marketing intelligence.

## Revenue Lines

| Line | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Consumer | Direct-to-consumer cybersecurity products |
| B2B Cyber | Enterprise cybersecurity and fraud detection services |
| AI SEO | AI-powered search engine optimization services |
| EA Services | Executive assistant and managed services |

## Marketing Channels

Paid Ads, Organic, Email, Referral, Partner

## Connected Tools

| Tool | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| HubSpot CRM | Contacts, deals, campaigns, pipeline data |
| Firecrawl | Web scraping, competitor analysis, SERP monitoring |
| Gmail | Ad receipts, campaign notifications, outreach tracking |
| Google Calendar | Campaign scheduling, review cadences |
| Google Drive | Invoice storage ("AfriShield Invoices" folder) |

## Skills

### weekly-marketing-report
Full GCPS pipeline (Gather, Contextualize, Process, Structure) that pulls data from HubSpot, Gmail, and Google Drive, benchmarks against African market data, computes ROAS/CAC/LTV per channel, and outputs a branded visual report.

### competitive-content-brief
Competitor content audit using Firecrawl scraping and SERP analysis. Scores content gaps, generates prioritized recommendations with effort/impact scoring, and outputs an actionable brief with topics, keywords, and content types.
